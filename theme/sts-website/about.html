About us

STS stands for Secret to Success. The secret is the part nobody wants to hear.

Stand by your goals. Stay passionate. Avoid shortcuts. And most importantly — do not give up, however many times the challenge repeats itself. That is the whole method, and it is why the institute carries the name it does.

Founded 2019

Built so that Narsingdi students would not have to leave Narsingdi

STS Institute was founded in 2019 to give people the skills they need to hold their own in a global environment. That started with English — and it stayed with English, because that is the door almost every other opportunity is behind.

Today we are a recognised leader in English language education in the district, with a range of programs wide enough to meet students where they actually are: from a school child sounding out phonics, to a working adult who needs band 7.0 for a visa in four months.

We do not sell shortcuts. If your English is not ready for IELTS, we will say so at the placement assessment and put you on Foundation instead. Students remember the institutes that told them the truth.

English Language Academy

The venue story

We asked. Then we showed them.

AUGUST 2023

The question

We asked IDP whether IELTS exams could be offered at our Narsingdi location. A reasonable question — and one that needed evidence, not enthusiasm.

31 DECEMBER 2023

The expo

We hosted an expo event and invited IDP to come and see it for themselves — the facilities, the systems, the students, and the plan we intended to run.

2024

The approval

The event led to official approval. STS Institute became an authorised IDP IELTS test venue, and Narsingdi students could finally register and test at home.

Two awards at the IDP IELTS Partners' Award Night 2025

Recognised in the Rising Category, and given a Special Award for Excellence in Innovation and Marketing. We remain committed to educational excellence, and to serving this community with whatever we can bring to it next.
