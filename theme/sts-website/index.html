Why trust us

Being a partner is one thing. Being chosen as a venue is another.

In August 2023 we asked IDP whether the IELTS exam could be hosted at our Narsingdi location. Talking about our facilities was not going to be enough, so on 31 December 2023 we ran an expo and invited them to walk through it — the rooms, the systems, the students, the plan.

Approval followed. Since 2024, Narsingdi students have been able to register for and sit IELTS in their own district. In 2025, IDP handed us two awards at the Partners' Award Night.

Beginner to success

Five steps, and we will tell you honestly which one you are on

Most students who fail IELTS started it too early. Our placement assessment puts you on the right step first — even when that means telling you to wait.

STEP 01

Placement

A free 25-minute assessment. We tell you your real level, not the one you hoped for.

STEP 02

Foundation

Grammar rebuilt for use, vocabulary systems, and a reading habit. Three months that save a year.

STEP 03

Skills training

All four IELTS skills, taught against the band descriptors you are actually marked on.

STEP 04

Mock & review

Four full mocks under exam conditions, each with a personal score breakdown session.

STEP 05

Test day

Sat at our own IDP venue, in the building you already know. Then result guidance.
